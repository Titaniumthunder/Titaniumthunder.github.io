RF Jamming-Detection CubeSat

Downlink verified
May – Jun 2026 · C · Salvo RTOS · dsPIC33 · I²C · AX.25 · GNU Radio · USRP SDR

Satellite-side firmware for an RF jamming-detection CubeSat. The mission: a Raspberry Pi payload detects jamming attacks, and the satellite downlinks the evidence (attack type, frequency, and GPS location) to a ground station. I own the full communications pipeline between the Pi payload, the dsPIC33 flight computer, and the EnduroSat UHF transceiver, building on a previous researcher's codebase.

  • Verified end-to-end downlink: real jamming-detection JSON travels from the Pi over I²C to the OBC and transmits via AX.25 at 435 MHz (9.6 kbaud FSK) to a GNU Radio ground station on a USRP N2920.
  • Concurrency design: multiple RTOS tasks (telemetry beacon, payload polling, uplink listener) share one I²C bus with a half-duplex transceiver. My binary-semaphore arbitration eliminated recurring freezes; the bug was the semaphore being released between 16-byte transfer chunks, letting other tasks corrupt messages mid-stream.
  • Cross-stack debugging: isolated three independent faults via single-variable testing: a shared-buffer race condition, a missing carriage-return terminator against the ESTTC protocol spec, and a blocking GPS serial read starving the main loop (fixed with a background daemon thread and cached fix).
  • Protocol & integrity: CRC-32-validated telemetry beacons per the EnduroSat ESTTC protocol; uplink command handling in progress.
